I²C address collision map

Every I²C part by address. Two parts at the same address cannot share a bus — you need a multiplexer, a second I²C bus on spare GPIOs, or a part whose address is strappable. Composite modules occupy several addresses at once; each one is listed. Plan the bus here before you order.
